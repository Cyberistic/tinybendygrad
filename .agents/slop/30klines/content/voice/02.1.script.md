### b1

A UOp is a dataclass with three fields. That is the entire intermediate representation, and the next three hours are built on it.

### b2

op is a member of a closed enum. src is a tuple of other UOps. So a node points down into a DAG, and the DAG is made of nothing but these three fields.

*cue:* 02.1.1
*lines:* 229, 230

*direction:* point at src with the cursor

### b3

src is a tuple, and tuples do not mutate. You never edit a node. You build a new one and let the cache collapse the duplicate. That single choice is why every rewrite in this codebase can be a pure function.

*cue:* 02.1.2
*lines:* 230

*direction:* pause after 'pure function'

### b4

There is no dtype field. dtype is a property computed from op, src and arg, then cached on the node. Store less and derive more — as long as the derivation always terminates.

### b5

And arg is Any. A constant, an axis, a device name, a reduction. op decides how to read it. It is the one place this IR gives up on types, and it is why the node stays at three fields.
