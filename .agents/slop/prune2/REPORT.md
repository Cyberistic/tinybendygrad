## 0. The measurement that almost lied

`.agents/slop` reported **1558 MB** on the first `os.walk` sum and **71 MB** under `du`.
The gap is `.agents/slop/e2epy/fixtures/*/sandbox/`: **196 symlinks to system binaries**
(`node`, `zsh`, …). `os.path.getsize` follows a symlink and returns the target's size, so the
walk charged e2epy **1495 MB** for 196 symlinks it does not own. `st_blocks`/`du` (lstat)
reports e2epy as **728 KB**. Every size below is `st_blocks`-based.

**The 84 MB shape of the last prune is NOT still here.** The five frozen port copies it removed
