## 1. WHY IT IS LOAD-BEARING, RESTATED AS ONE MEASUREMENT

`.agents/slop/browser/arenas.bend` dumps one fixture in two arenas:

    A_ranged_dump=STORE:none:INDEX,REDUCE      <- 22 nodes, the arithmetic IS there
    C_sched_dump =STORE:none:INDEX,PARAM       <- 10 nodes, it is GONE

`.agents/slop/browser/d1arity.bend` names the cause in two rows:

    D1_a_store_before=STORE(INDEX,REDUCE)  store_after_replace=STORE(INDEX,PARAM)
    D1_b_call_before=CALL(STORE,PARAM)     call_after_replace=CALL(STORE,PARAM)

`replace` (`schedule/__init__.bend:1304`) is correct and **only its subject is
wrong**. Upstream's loop asserts the subject first:

    tinygrad/schedule/__init__.py:75   assert k.op is Ops.CALL, f"unexpected op in queue: {k.op}"

The port's `replace` header says *"`k` is a CALL here -- `__init__.py:75 asserts
it"* and the code does not check it. So a STORE is rewritten as if it were a
CALL: `src[0]` (the INDEX) is kept, `src[1:]` (`[REDUCE]`) becomes
`sc_buf_uop(REDUCE)` = a PARAM. **The assertion became a comment.**

*(The `d1arity.bend` header cites `schedule/__init__.bend:1063` for `replace`.
The def is at `:1304` on this tree. A citation that names a line it does not
own is the class this tree keeps finding.)*

## 4. WHY THE ENGINE CANNOT RUN STEP 2 AS IT STANDS (TWO INDEPENDENT REASONS)

`egraph_rewrite` is not the obstacle. The two obstacles are stated in the
engine's own header and both are measurable:

1. **`bottom_up` is not expressible.** `codegen/__init__.bend:400-405`:
   *"the port has ONE table and both drivers read it top-down, so
   `bottom_up=True` is not expressible and this signature does not carry it."*
   The split's collection order IS the PARAM slot order (`enumerate(lctx.map)`),
   so top-down would number the slots in the wrong order.
2. **`ctx` cannot be a mutable pair of maps.** `O.CTX` (`uop/ops.bend:2666`) has
   `CArgs{xs}` and `CNodes{m}` — both immutable, and a `CNodes` map has no
   insertion order. `LocalAddBufferContext` needs `map` AND `ranges`, both
   insertion-ordered, both written by rules, plus `BottomUpGate` to stop the
   descent at a buffer. The scheduler already solves the same problem its own
   way: `Ctx.degs` is a `List<Dep>` (`schedule/__init__.bend`, `zero`/`z_go`),
   *because a map does not preserve insertion order.*

**So step 2 is either (a) a named bottom-up driver for this pass that carries
its own `List<(&2,U32)>` context, or (b) a cross-cutting engine change to carry
an ordered mutable ctx and a bottom-up table. (a) is smaller and is what upstream
optimises for anyway: `split_store` runs `graph_rewrite` on a KERNEL-SIZED graph.**

## 5. `debuf` IS BLOCKED ON ONE PRIMITIVE, AND THE PRIMITIVE IS GENERAL

`debuf` (`rangeify.py:283-290`):

    param = UOp.param(slot, buf.dtype, buf.max_numel(), addrspace=..., device=...)
    ret = param.reshape(buf.max_shape)
    if buf.max_shape != buf.shape: ret = ret.shrink(...)

| piece | port | status |
|---|---|---|
| `UOp.param(slot, dtype, n, ...)` | `UOp.new.param` (`uop/ops.bend:1983`) — takes `n: Maybe<U32>`, builds `src[0]=shape_to_shape_arg((prod,))` | **present** |
| `buf.max_numel()` | `ParamArg.size` (`uop/ops.bend:1993`) reads `src[0]` | **present** |
| `buf.max_shape` / `buf.shape` | `UOp.shape` (`uop/fold.bend:2846`) via the fold | **present** |
| `param.reshape(max_shape)` | **`UOp.reshape` does not exist.** Movement exists only at the TENSOR level: `G.reshape` (`mixin/movement.bend:963`, 4-long shape only), `mxw_reshape` (`:1353`), `mo_reshape` (`mixin/op.bend:706`) | **MISSING** |

## 6. THE THREE UNITS, IN DEPENDENCY ORDER

1. **`UOp.view_as` / `UOp.reshape`** (`ops.py:1201`, `:1170`) — the general
   primitive. Exit: a reshape of the same shape answers `self`; a flat PARAM
   reshaped to `(4,3)` answers a RESHAPE whose shape fold is `(4,3)`; a symbolic
   shape refuses by name. Consumers: `debuf`, `mint`, `alloc`, `postrange`.
2. **`debuf`** (`rangeify.py:283`) — PARAM at a kernel-local slot, flat numel,
   reshaped to `max_shape`. Exit: node-for-node against CPython's `debuf` on the
   port's own PARAMs, with a plant that moves the slot and one that moves the
   shape.
3. **`split_store`** (`rangeify.py:326`) — the ordered two-map context, the
   bottom-up walk over the kernel-sized graph, `substitute`, renumber, and
   `CALL(SINK(body, KernelInfo), *bufs)`. Exit: `arenas.bend`'s `C` row keeps
   `STORE(INDEX,REDUCE)` after `schedule`, and `linearize` answers the arithmetic
   nodes; `fullchain`'s 198 bytes become 547.

**A partial landing of (3) is the failure mode to refuse**: a CALL whose body
PARAM slots are the original global slots matches the CALL's src positions only
by coincidence, and a coincidence is a row that cannot go red. Upstream renumbers
for exactly this reason (`rangeify.py:318`: *"so that kernel deduping works"*).

**And the assert, while (3) is unlanded, is a separate decision**: porting
`assert k.op is Ops.CALL` as a refusal makes `arenas.bend`'s `C` row go from
`STORE(INDEX,PARAM)` to *no schedule* — honest, and it removes the only kernel
the port currently produces. That is a capability trade, not a fix, and it
should be made deliberately rather than as a side effect.

## 8. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT CLAIM

- It does not claim step 2 is impossible — it claims the engine as it stands cannot
  express it, with the two lines that say so.
- It does not claim `F.UOp.ranges` gives the right answer on a rangeified graph; it
  claims the *primitive exists and is used*, and that `rf_in_ranges` has no caller.
  The right answer is one probe row (`Rng.rs` + `Rng.ok` + the axis of each range),
  and it is the first thing unit 3 should print.
- It does not claim the 484 kb bundle is the whole port; it is the closure of
  `renderer/wgsl.bend` (2 429 defs), and the full `wgsl_walk` closure is 3 333.
