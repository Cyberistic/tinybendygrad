# live-write-registry

A harness MAY write into `tinybendygrad/` only if it has a line here.

**The registry is EMPTY, and that is the finding.**  27 harnesses in
`.agents/slop/` name a live-source write and none of them is registered.  Run
`python3 .agents/slop/live-write-guard.py` to see them; rc=1 until they are fixed.

## WHY AN EMPTY FILE IS BETTER THAN A LOOSE ONE

The rule is not "write to a snapshot" and it is not "take a digest first".  Both have
been tried in this corpus and both are skippable by a tired agent.  The rule is:

    A harness may not NAME a write into `tinybendygrad/`, unless it is registered here.

`staged_mut.Staged` needs no entry: its only destination is the staged copy beside the
target, so its AST cannot name a live path at all.  Four harnesses were converted on
2026-10-04 (`ops-501-mutate.py`, and the two this unit did, plus `nv_mutate.py`) and
none of them appears below.

## FORMAT

One line, four fields, and the reason must name WHAT WOULD BREAK, not what the harness
intends:

    <harness.py>  <the destination or spelling>  <what a run would destroy>  <YYYY-MM-DD>

## A LINE THAT IS NOT A JUSTIFICATION

    bad-mutate.py  SRC  runs safely    2026-10-04

That line is refused by review rather than by the guard, because the guard cannot tell a
justification from a placeholder.  What a justification must contain:

* the CONCURRENT-WRITE window -- who else edits that file, and what their work is;
* what the harness does instead of writing (a scratch copy, a mirror, a read);
* what happens on the SECOND run if the first one's write landed.

`blob-intern-mutate.py`'s own docstring is the worked example of a justification that
was believed and was wrong: its `finally` restore put a dead 6,623-line `ops.bend` over
the live 6,306-line one, so "it restores the snapshot" is not a reason for anything.
