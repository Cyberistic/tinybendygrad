interning: the node carries the op's number, and the comparison is O(1)

`Ops.value` IS A SIXTY-ARM MATCH, SO A CALL COSTS THE POSITION OF ITS ARM, AND THE INTERNING
ASKED FOR IT 21,325,576 TIMES A TURN (MEASURED IN THE COMPILED FORM,
`.agents/slop/wgslperf/count-intern.py` COUNTS THE CALLS AND THE ARM POSITIONS: THE MEAN
POSITION WAS 37.8 OF ~60, AND THE TOP TWELVE ARMS WERE ~79% OF THE CALLS).

THE NODE NOW CARRIES THE OP's NUMBER, SO THE COMPARISON IS A FIELD READ. THE NUMBER IS COMPUTED
ONCE, AT THE NODE's CONSTRUCTION, AND THE OP's VARIANTS ARE UNTOUCHED -- THE OP TYPE HAS 4,833
PATTERN SITES ACROSS 65 FILES, AND THE NODE HAS 23. THE VALUES DO NOT MOVE: EACH OP KEEPS ITS
OWN NUMBER, AND THE NUMBERS ARE THE DECLARATION ORDER, WHICH THE PORT DEPENDS ON
(`schedule/rangeify.bend` SORTS OPS BY IT, AND CPython AGREES).

THE COMPARISON IS NOW `U32.is_eq(ov, ov2)` AND THE SCAN NEVER CALLS `Ops.value` AT ALL: THE
WANTED NODE's NUMBER IS IN THE NODE, AND SO IS EVERY OTHER NODE's.

MEASURED, THE WGSL TURN (`.agents/slop/wgslperf/bench-ab.py`, WHICH BUILDS BOTH TREES NOW AND
ALTERNATES THEM, SO A DRIFT IN THE SHARED MACHINE HITS BOTH): 1,275 ms -> 1,162 ms OF WALL AND
1,801 ms -> 1,711 ms OF CPU. THE WGSL IS BYTE-IDENTICAL: 3,773 bytes, THE SAME SHA256, FROM
BOTH BUILDS.

AND THE GATES ARE GREEN: `gates/ops-core-gate.py`, `gates/fold-vbase-gate.py`,
`gates/specfold-gate.py`, `gates/decomp_graph_census-gate.py`, `gates/ew_graph_census-gate.py`
AND `gates/gr_graph_census-gate.py`.

RUNS AGAIN ON THE SETTLED TREE.
