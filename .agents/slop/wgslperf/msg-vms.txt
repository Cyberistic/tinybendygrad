vms: the fuel is the arena's budget, and it is computed once

THE VMS IS THE LINEARIZER's VALUE MAP, AND EVERY WALK IN IT COMPUTED `O.Arena.budget(ar)` --
WHICH IS A FOLD OVER EVERY NODE OF THE ARENA AND OVER EVERY SRC OF EVERY NODE. SO EVERY WALK
PAID FOR A WHOLE-ARENA FOLD, AND THE VMS WALKS THE ARENA PER ELEMENT.

THE BUDGET IS THE SAME FOR EVERY WALK: THE ARENA DOES NOT GROW WHILE THE VMS IS COMPUTED
(EVERY WALK IN IT IS A READER -- `UOp.toposort` IS A WALK, `Arena.src_from` IS A READ, AND
`vms_cons`/`vms_ends`/`vms_u`/`vms_enc`/`vms_row` ARE READERS). SO THE FUEL IS NOW COMPUTED
ONCE, AT THE TOP OF THE VMS, AND CARRIED DOWN.

MEASURED, THE WGSL TURN (`.agents/slop/wgslperf/bench-ab.py`, WHICH BUILDS BOTH TREES NOW AND
ALTERNATES THEM, SO A DRIFT IN THE SHARED MACHINE HITS BOTH): 1,072 ms -> 1,042 ms OF WALL AND
1,604 ms -> 1,560 ms OF CPU. THE WGSL IS BYTE-IDENTICAL: 3,773 bytes, THE SAME SHA256, FROM
BOTH BUILDS.

`gates/devtag-gate.py`, `gates/fold-vbase-gate.py`, `gates/specfold-gate.py`,
`gates/decomp_graph_census-gate.py`, `gates/ew_graph_census-gate.py` AND
`gates/gr_graph_census-gate.py`. AND THE THREE THAT DO NOT PASS AT `HEAD` -- `devset-census-gate`,
`realize_graph_census-gate` AND `phase-taxonomy-gate` -- PRINT BYTE-IDENTICAL OUTPUT BEFORE AND
AFTER THIS CHANGE, SO THEY ARE PRE-EXISTING AND NOT THIS CHANGE.

RUNS AGAIN ON THE SETTLED TREE.
