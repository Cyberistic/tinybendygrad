V8's OWN CPU PROFILE OF THE COMPILED FORM NAMES THE COST: `intern.find.go` IS 16.1% OF THE
TURN's EXCLUSIVE TIME, AND IT IS THE WALK OF THE WHOLE ARENA. THE OLD FORM SCANNED EVERY NODE
AND THEN CHOSE THE HEAD, SO IT COULD NOT STOP: 20,818,516 NODE COMPARISONS A TURN OVER 85,340
SCANS OF AN ARENA OF ~244 NODES.

MEASURED, THE MATCH IS NOT AT THE END: THE HISTOGRAM OF THE MATCH'S INDEX (THE COMPILED FORM
COUNTS IT, `.agents/slop/wgslperf/count-intern.py`) PUTS THE MEAN AT 147.7 OF ~244, AND 79% OF
THE SCANS FIND A MATCH AT ALL. SO THE SCAN WALKED ~40% MORE NODES THAN IT NEEDED TO.

THE SCAN NOW STOPS AT THE MATCH, AND THE MATCH IS THE HEAD's VERDICT, WHICH THE CALLER
COMPUTES: A `match` CANNOT SCRUTINIZE A COMPUTED VALUE, SO THE VERDICT IS A PARAMETER AND THE
CALLER COMPARES THE NEXT NODE. THE ORDER OF THE `match`es IS THE TREE's OWN RULE: THE LIST
FIRST, THEN THE LIST's TAIL, THEN THE VERDICT (A `match` ON A LATER BINDER CANNOT FOLLOW A
`match` ON AN EARLIER ONE).

MEASURED, THE WGSL TURN (`.agents/slop/wgslperf/bench-ab.py`, WHICH BUILDS BOTH TREES NOW AND
ALTERNATES THEM, SO A DRIFT IN THE SHARED MACHINE HITS BOTH): 1,586 ms -> 1,309 ms OF WALL AND
2,137 ms -> 1,853 ms OF CPU. THE WGSL IS BYTE-IDENTICAL: 3,773 bytes, THE SAME SHA256, FROM
BOTH BUILDS.
