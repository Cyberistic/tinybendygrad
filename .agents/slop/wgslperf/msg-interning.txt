interning: the op comparison is halved, and the turn is 20.9% faster

V8's OWN CPU PROFILE OF THE COMPILED FORM NAMES THE COST: `Ops.value` IS 13.1% OF THE
TURN's EXCLUSIVE TIME, AND `eq_arg`, `eq_u32` AND `eq_tag` ARE ANOTHER 8.4% -- AND ALL OF IT
IS ONE FUNCTION, THE INTERNING's NODE COMPARISON.

THE MEASUREMENT, FROM THE COMPILED FORM (`.agents/slop/wgslperf/count-intern.py` COUNTS IT):
  intern.find      20,818,516 NODE COMPARISONS, OVER 85,340 SCANS OF AN ARENA OF ~244 NODES
  Ops.value        41,973,508 CALLS, AND ITS SIXTY ARMS ARE 1,684,550,390 TAG COMPARISONS
  eq_arg           20,735,255 CALLS          eq_u32   40,105,790 CALLS
  eq_tag           20,735,255 CALLS

TWO CAUSES, AND BOTH ARE IN `eq_node`:

1. THE COMPARISON ASKED FOR `Ops.value` OF BOTH OPS, AND THE WANTED NODE's OP IS FIXED FOR THE
   WHOLE SCAN. THE OP VALUE IS NOW HOISTED OUT OF THE SCAN AND CARRIED, SO THE SCAN ASKS FOR
   ONE `Ops.value` PER NODE INSTEAD OF TWO.

2. `Bool.and` EVALUATES BOTH OF ITS ARGUMENTS, SO THE OLD FORM COMPARED THE SRCS, THE ARG AND
   THE TAG ON EVERY NODE -- EVEN WHEN THE OPS DIFFERED AND THE ANSWER WAS ALREADY FALSE. THEY
   ARE NOW COMPARED ONLY WHEN THE OP MATCHES.

THE SAME COUNTS AFTER:
  Ops.value        21,325,576 CALLS, AND ITS ARMS ARE 801,275,119 TAG COMPARISONS
  eq_arg            1,136,865 CALLS          eq_u32    2,691,180 CALLS
  eq_tag            1,136,865 CALLS

MEASURED, THE WGSL TURN (`.agents/slop/wgslperf/bench-ab.py`, WHICH BUILDS BOTH TREES NOW AND
ALTERNATES THEM, SO A DRIFT IN THE SHARED MACHINE HITS BOTH): 3,725 ms -> 2,946 ms OF WALL AND
4,228 ms -> 3,461 ms OF CPU. THE PHASE CLOCKS AGREE: `rr_run_rangeify` 1,218 ms -> 691 ms.

AND THE WGSL IS BYTE-IDENTICAL: 3,773 bytes, THE SAME SHA256, FROM BOTH BUILDS.

THE `eq_node` THAT `key_same` USES IS UNTOUCHED, AND `gates/ops-core-gate.py` IS GREEN (45
ROWS, THREE LANES). THE CORPUS ARTIFACT ON THIS TREE READS 38 OF 38 GRAPHS ANSWERED, 38
AGREE, NONE DISAGREE, 38 BYTE-IDENTICAL, expect-moved=0, PLANTS 8 OF 8, CONFLATIONS 4 OF 4,
CONTROLS 5 OF 5, AND ITS SUBSTRATE IS THIS TREE's.

ONE THING THIS DOES NOT FIX, AND IT IS MEASURED: THE SCAN STILL WALKS THE WHOLE ARENA, SO THE
EARLY EXIT IS STILL ON THE TABLE (IT NEEDS A MATCH ON A COMPUTED VALUE, WHICH BEND REFUSES).
