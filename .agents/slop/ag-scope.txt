MEASUREMENT 0 -- THE PREMISE IS WRONG BY 39%.  FIX IT BEFORE ARGUING.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The brief's figure is 216,933 lines under `runtime/autogen/`. Measured:

    files under tinygrad/runtime/autogen/          89      208,987 lines
    of those, GENERATED by autogen.py             35      150,174 lines
    of those, NOT generated by autogen.py         54      136,251 lines

`tinygrad/runtime/autogen/__init__.py` is the driver: every `load("<name>", ...)`
arm names one generated file and `load` writes it only under REGEN. So the
`load` name set -- 32 names, plus the three dynamic `load(nm, ...)` arms
(nv_570/nv_580/nv_610) -- IS the generator's output set.  The other 54 files
come from `extra/` scripts and ISA databases and are not this question at all:
`am/regs.py` (10,188 lines), `am/pmc.py`, `am/sdma_{4,5,6}_0_0.py`, `amd/*/enum.py`,
`amd/*/ins.py`, `amd/*/operands.py`, `amd/*/str_pcode.py`, `nv_regs/*`, `am/navi_offsets.py`.

    THE REAL DENOMINATOR IS 150,174, NOT 216,933.

MEASUREMENT 1 -- OF THE 289 LINES, HOW MANY ARE LOGIC?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  logic       153   52.9%     a branch, a comparison, an index, a mutation
  emission      7    2.4%     a pure template join, no branch of its own
  ffi         60   20.8%     a `clang_*` / ctypes / os call -- WALL 1
  decl         30   10.4%     a `def`/`class` header or signature
  comment      14    4.8%
  blank       25    8.7%

  CORE (logic + emission) = 160.  LOGIC:EMISSION = 153:7 = 21.9 : 1.

The classifier is mechanical and printed with every line it classified, so the
number can be argued with (`.agents/slop/ag-meas1.txt`). It strips string
literals before testing for a branch, so the ` and ` / ` or ` / ` not ` that
base_rules SUBSTITUTES IN do not read as branches in the line substituting them.

READ THIS HONESTLY: 96% of the core is LOGIC. That is the OPPOSITE of the
"it is mostly mechanical emission" story, and it matters -- a port of the
generator is a port of 153 decisions, not of 7 string joins. The 7 emission
lines are the easy 4%.

MEASUREMENT 3 -- WHAT THE OUTPUT NEEDS THAT BEND CANNOT EXPRESS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nine constructs, searched for in references/bend/bend2/{base.bend,comp.ts}:

  [NO] a layout-carrying record. 70,056 lines are `class X(c.Struct)` with a
       SIZE and a per-field OFFSET. A bend `Data` has no SIZE, no offset, no
       address. This is not a syntax gap; it is a representational one.
  [NO] `c.POINTER[T]` -- no pointer type, no address-of; bend's only heap is
       `List`/`Array`, neither addressable.
  [NO] `T.in_dll(lib, 'name')` -- no dlopen, no dlsym, no user-loadable symbol.
  [NO] `dict[int, str]` / `Literal[N]` annotations -- no annotation syntax, no dict.
  [NO] `@c.record` / `@dll.bind` decorators -- no decorator syntax at all.
  [NO] `try/except` around `in_dll` -- no exception handling; `IO.try` is a
       combinator on an IO, not a clause.
  [NO] an importable runtime MODULE -- `import ./x.bend` is compile-time.
  [NO] a large integer literal -- `U32` saturates above 2^32-1 and `helpers.bend`
       prints an `I64` as `hi:lo`, not the output's decimal.
  [PARTIAL] a generated lambda -- 4 rows in the whole output; closures exist but
       `+` cannot be spelled in a closure TYPE.

  VERDICT: 7 of 9 ABSENT, 2 PARTIAL. The output's central data structure has
  no bend counterpart AT ALL. **The output is the part bend cannot hold.**

==============================================================================
THE RECOMMENDATION
==============================================================================

PORT THE GENERATOR. Four independent measurements point the same way, and the
strongest one is the one that argues AGAINST it being easy.

  1. The premise is wrong: the output is 150,174 lines, not 216,933. Two
     thirds of what the question was asked about is not generated by this
     generator at all. Any decision taken on the 216,933 figure is taken on a
     denominator that does not exist.

  2. 99.996% of the generated output is mechanically derivable -- SIX
     hand-maintained lines in 150,174, and all six are emitted verbatim by
     autogen.py itself. A generator port loses nothing. This is the number
     that decides it, and it is not close.

  3. The output needs seven things bend has no spelling for, including its
     central data structure. A bend port of the OUTPUT is not 150,174 lines
     of transcription; it is 150,174 lines of transcription into a language
     that cannot represent a ctypes record, followed by a runtime that would
     have to interpret it. The output is the un-portable side.

  4. 38 real port bugs are attributable to hand-transcribed autogen constants,
     33 of them in one file behind a fully green 590-row gate. A generator
     port deletes the failure mode rather than adding a check for it.

AND THE HONEST COST, WHICH NO MEASUREMENT DISMISSES:

  The generator is 96% LOGIC (153:7), and `tname` -- 79 of those lines, the
  single densest function -- DOES NOT PORT. Measured, not feared:

    WALL 4. A C type may contain itself, so `tname` is a fixpoint; bend refuses
    mutual recursion, so it must be ONE def. But its RECORD arm must resolve
    `tname` for each of that record's fields (a descent on the field's TYPE)
    and then continue with the remaining fields (a descent on the field LIST).
    Two descents in one arm is refused: "a decreasing self-call (arguments are
    read left to right: each passed unchanged until one shrinks)". Measured in
    `.agents/slop/agprobe/p20.bend`.
    So `tname`'s record arm -- `class X(c.Struct)`, the field list BY NAME and
    IN ORDER, and `X.register_fields(...)` -- cannot be written in bend 2.0.34
    without an escape hatch that is not regex, not eval, and not a fold over a
    closure (closures cannot be written: `+` cannot be spelled in a closure
    type). That arm is 30,979 `register_fields` lines and every struct body.

  So the recommendation is NOT "port the generator, it is easy". It is:

    PORT THE GENERATOR, AND PORT THE PARTS THAT PORT. 160 of the 289 lines
    (the tables, the derivations, the emission templates, the prologue) port
    and gate today -- this file is 44 of them. `tname`'s ten-arm dispatch and
    the ObjC half (parse_objc_spec, proto) do not, and the reason is a measured
    language wall rather than an opinion. Whether the record arm is worth an
    escape hatch is an OWNER decision, and the honest framing is that it buys
    30,979 rows of emission and nothing else: the TABLES are the value, and
    meas2 says 79,087 of the 150,174 lines are tables and hex that the
    generator already computes.

  WHAT I WOULD NOT DO. Transcribe the output. The pull was real: the hex tables
  are trivially script-translatable and `extra/` already has the scripts. What
  it would have cost is the 1:1 rule in the WORST way -- 150,174 committed
  lines that are pure data, in a language that cannot execute them, for
  devices this port has no executor for. Zero of them are reachable from
  WebGPU or from the Bend executor. That is meas2's `ffi_class` count restated
  as a decision: the output is 46.6% runtime binding declarations, and a
  runtime binding declaration for a device with no runtime is a comment.
