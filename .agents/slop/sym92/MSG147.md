`s1.multi` is the walk that consults the whole table, and `s1.find` is the lookup it needs:

    s1.get(tbl, k)   returns the replacement FOR k, DEFAULTING TO k

**THE DEFAULT IS WHY `s1.get` CANNOT ANSWER THE FIRST QUESTION**: "absent" and "present, mapped to
itself" are the same answer, and a table that maps a key to ITSELF is legitimate (upstream's
`substitute` may be given `{X: X}`), so the default cannot be overloaded to mean "absent". `s1.find`
returns a `Maybe` and the replacement comes WITH it.
