Upstream guards 19 sites in `symbolic.py` with two predicates:

added an hour ago -- and ZERO `no_overflow`. The other rules were ported WITHOUT their guard, which
is the DANGEROUS direction: a guard that is missing makes a rule FIRE where CPython declines, so the
port rewrites a graph CPython leaves alone.

FIXED HERE, the first of them:

    symbolic.py:253  (x*c0)+(x*c1) -> x*(c0+c1)   guard: fits(x, c0.vmin+c1.vmin)

`sym_245` minted `x*(c0+c1)` unconditionally. The coefficient sum is built at the CONSTS' dtype
while the product is taken at the OPERAND's, so an `x` that cannot hold `c0+c1` was rewritten into a
product whose own coefficient does not fit.

VERIFIED, both cases, int8 (`[-128, 127]`):

    (x*1)+(x*2)          -> (mul x (add c1 c2))              FIRES
    (x*127)+(x*127)      -> (add (mul x c127) (mul x c127))  DECLINES, unchanged

The second line is the whole point: `127+127` is 254 and int8 cannot hold it, so CPython's `fits`
refuses and now the port does too. **A GUARD THAT NEVER REFUSES IS A LABEL, and this one refuses.**

The CPython half of `probe-253.py` returns `None` from `sym.rewrite` for a RAW-built node (the mixin
path in `probe-swap.py` works), so the port side is the evidence here and the guard's semantics are
taken from the line itself. That limitation is written down rather than papered over.
